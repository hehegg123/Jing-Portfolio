Literature review

Barriers to automated vehicle adoption for older adults

Reviewing a decade of research on factors that shape older adults’ acceptance of automated vehicles.

Research question

What barriers do older adults face when adopting automated vehicles, and what could vehicle designers learn from those barriers?

Approach

We reviewed a decade of research to identify factors influencing older adults’ acceptance of automated vehicles and consider how future designs could address their needs.

Outcome

We presented the review at the 68th Human Factors and Ergonomics Society Annual Meeting (ASPIRE).
