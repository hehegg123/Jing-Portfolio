Work zone safety

Evaluating automated flagger assistance devices

Comparing systems that can reduce flaggers’ exposure to traffic while maintaining work zone traffic control.

Problem

Flaggers often work close to live traffic. Automated flagger assistance devices can help move workers away from that exposure while still directing vehicles through the work zone.

Approach

We evaluate existing automated flagger assistance devices across different operating environments. The Indiana Department of Transportation funds this project.
