Hi, I'm Jing! 👋

I'm a Human Factors and UX Researcher and a Ph.D. candidate at Purdue University.

I believe great technology starts with understanding people: not just what they say they want, but what they truly need and how they actually behave. Through user research, human-centered design, and experimental evaluation, I explore how people interact with intelligent and automated systems, uncover user needs, and design and evaluate interaction strategies to make interactions with technology safer, more efficient, effortless, and engaging.

Nice to meet you here! Feel free to explore my website and check out some of the projects I have been working on!
